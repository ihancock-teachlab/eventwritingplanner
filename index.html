Seed Story
For teachers
  • Built on Adrienne Gear's Event Story structure (Powerful Writing Structures, Memory Pocket Writing): a real event, small enough to start and end in one day.
  • 8-box planner, filled in Gear's order: beginning (Box 1) and ending (Box 8) first, then the middle (Box 4), then Boxes 2, 3, 5, 6, 7. Each box starts with a move-along word; "Suddenly" is left out because it signals a problem.
  • Box 8 is built in three parts: move-along word + what happened, Topic + Feeling = Ending, then a big thought (a lesson or why it matters).
  • Zoom In follows Stretching the Moment with the six senses (feelings are the sixth). The hook step adapts Hook Your Reader.
  • One question per screen. Help (ideas, starters, examples) stays hidden until the student taps "Help me." Students can also choose to fill in the plan themselves from one page and open any part.
  • Plans save in the student's browser only. The Word file opens in Google Docs for Classroom.
